# CIS428 Metaverse Technology

Assignment 2

## **<u>Description</u>**

This is an individual assignment.

You are employed as a metaverse content creator. Your company has been commissioned to design a new interactive metaverse experience that introduces an original character IP while remaining clearly connected to an existing brand or story IP (e.g., a well-known film franchise, game universe, sports brand, lifestyle brand, or literary property).

Your task is to create a cohesive metaverse experience that feels like a natural extension or creative “what-if” spin-off of the chosen existing IP, while showcasing a fully original character (or small cast of original characters) that you design and own.

## **<u>Project Brief</u>**

Core Requirement:

- Create a new metaverse experience featuring one or more original character IPs that exist in meaningful relation to an existing brand or story IP.

Key expectations:

- Original Character IP: Design a new character (or small set of characters) with distinctive visual design, personality, backstory, and role. **The character must be original — not a direct copy or minor recolor of an existing character.**

- Relation to Existing IP: The experience and character must clearly reference, inhabit, expand, or playfully reimagine an existing brand or story universe. **The connection should be intentional and recognizable**

- Interactive Experience: Build a playable land/experience in The Sandbox GameMaker that lets users interact with your original character(s) and the chosen IP world.

- Cohesive Narrative & Design: Visual style, interaction design, and narrative should feel consistent and purposeful.

## **<u>Tasks</u>**

1. Create the original game character(s) on VoxEdit

Design and model at least one original character that will serve as the hero, guide, rival, or mascot of your experience. Ensure the character has a clear visual identity and a short written backstory that links it to the chosen existing IP.

1. Create your own land with interactive experience using The Sandbox GameMaker

Build an interactive experience (quest, mini-game, exploration zone, brand activation space, etc.) that features your original character(s) and meaningfully engages with the existing brand/story IP. The experience should be playable and demonstrate thoughtful interaction design.

1. Prepare a 5-minute PowerPoint presentation

Present your concept, character design rationale, relationship to the existing IP, and a walkthrough of the interactive experience. Each student will present in class.

## **<u>Sample Idea Examples</u>**

The following are illustrative examples only. You are expected to develop their own original concepts.

## **1. Pokémon × Original Trainer-Companion Character**

- Existing IP: Pokémon

- Original Character IP: “Lumo” – a newly discovered dual-type (Electric/Fairy) creature that forms a symbiotic bond with a human trainer through shared dreams.

- Experience Concept: A dream-world island in The Sandbox where players help Lumo recover fragmented memories. Players solve light-based puzzles, catch “dream fragments,” and unlock story moments that expand the Pokémon lore in a non-canon, original direction.

## **2. Nike × Original Athlete-Mascot Character**

- Existing IP: Nike (brand)

- Original Character IP: “Aether” – a non-binary, gravity-defying parkour athlete whose signature move is the “Sky-Stride.” Aether wears evolving Nike-inspired digital gear that changes with player achievements.

- Experience Concept: An urban vertical playground land where players race and free-run with Aether. Completing challenges unlocks limited digital Nike collab wearables and a short cinematic about Aether’s origin story as a street athlete who “broke the sky.”

## **3. Studio Ghibli × Original Spirit Character**

- Existing IP: Studio Ghibli universe / aesthetic (e.g., Spirited Away, My Neighbor Totoro)

- Original Character IP: “Mochi-no-Kami” – a shy rice-spirit who accidentally swallows a forgotten childhood memory and must return it before the next full moon.

- Experience Concept: A gentle, hand-crafted village land filled with quiet exploration, seasonal changes, and small acts of kindness. Players help Mochi-no-Kami gather offerings and restore forgotten places, unlocking soft narrative vignettes that evoke Ghibli’s tone without copying any existing characters or plots.
